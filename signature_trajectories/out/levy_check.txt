The identity is exact for every channel (correlation 1.000): S^{t,j}/dj is the
increment-weighted mean position at which channel j moves. For a channel that
only increases that reads directly as 'how late does j accumulate'. For a
channel whose increments change sign (log_out_size, is_write on the raw path)
the same number is a signed weighted average that can sit outside [0,1] and no
longer has a plain-language reading.
